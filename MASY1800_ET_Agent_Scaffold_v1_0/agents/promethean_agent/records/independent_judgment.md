# Independent Professional Judgment

I would classify deployed generative AI as Potential Promethean, with moderate confidence in that provisional judgment. It can produce flexible responses without a person composing each one. Evidence from work and learning makes effects beyond one industry plausible. However, the sources do not establish general tenfold gains or permanent dependence across multiple tiers of life. I would not call it Confirmed Promethean.

For the hypothetical bank, I would approve a small internal summary pilot using public documents and analyst review. The relevant question is whether it saves time after checking the output, not whether AI is historically important. I would defer autonomous client recommendations because the case provides no validated safeguards. A hotel caption trial is easier to justify because staff can check short drafts before publication.

I would reconsider the broad classification if independent evidence demonstrated durable cross-tier barrier removal and infrastructure dependence. I would lower my confidence if benefits stayed narrow or largely disappeared after verification costs. The same broad classification should therefore support different actions in different contexts.
