# Independent Professional Judgment

I initially thought indoor autonomous delivery robots might be Potential Promethean because they can deliver items without someone accompanying every trip. When I discussed this with ChatGPT, the point that restaurants could return to manual delivery changed my view: the robots reduce the need for people to accompany deliveries, but do not dissolve the underlying barrier. I now classify them as Breakthrough, with moderate confidence. People still load items, receive deliveries, and handle problems, and the current evidence does not establish fundamental change across multiple tiers of human life.

For the large restaurant, I would support a small pilot after a route assessment. For the hotel, I would verify elevator access and recovery before starting multi-floor service. I would defer the small restaurant's proposed purchase because its assumed 55 cm bottleneck is below the proposed model's published 65 cm clearance. That finding concerns this model and layout, not every delivery robot.

The evidence is mainly supplier material, so I would measure total staff effort rather than accept savings claims. I would reconsider the broad classification if independent evidence showed durable cross-tier barrier removal and essential systems becoming dependent on the technology.
