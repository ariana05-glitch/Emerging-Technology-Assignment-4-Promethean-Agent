# Evidence register

Snapshot October 22, 2026. Only primary material is used; capability evidence is chiefly supplier-authored and has not been independently benchmarked. Organization facts are hypothetical. No market-size, price or ROI figures are asserted.

E1 | Fortino, Chapter 11, Identifying Promethean Technologies, August 2026 draft Rev 0.1, sections 11.2 and 11.7, pp. 3-4 and 8-11; supplied course PDF.
August 2026 draft; read 2026-10-22. Course framework. Chapter 11 distinguishes constraint reduction from categorical dissolution and requires the system, suppression and tier tests.
Limit: Conceptual lens, not empirical performance evidence.

E2 | https://www.pudurobotics.com/en/products/bellabotpro
Undated live product page; accessed 2026-10-22. Manufacturer product specification. PUDU describes BellaBot Pro delivery modes, obstacle sensing and a 65 cm path-clearance specification.
Limit: Supplier claim, not independent safety or ROI evidence. The page labels dish recognition and broadcast as under development; do not treat that feature as deployed.

E3 | https://www.pudurobotics.com/about/news/652128195da24800447b01c3
2023-09-22; accessed 2026-10-22. Manufacturer report of integration testing. PUDU reports a FlashBot elevator-integration test with Nippon Otis.
Limit: Historical supplier report, not proof of compatibility with an arbitrary hotel elevator or independent long-run service performance.

E4 | https://www.bearrobotics.ai/s/Servi-Plus_User-Manual_EN.pdf
Version 1.1.1; copyright 2023; accessed 2026-10-22. Manufacturer operating manual. The Servi Plus manual requires operating-space checks, mapping, human handoff and monitoring during peak periods.
Limit: Read safety and use sections, printed pp. 6, 10, 16 and 19. Model-specific limits do not transfer automatically to PUDU products; supports a bounded example of continuing human responsibilities.

E5 | https://docs.nav2.org/rolling/configuration_and_development/tuning_guide/
Live maintainer documentation; accessed 2026-10-22. Original software-maintainer documentation. Nav2 documentation explains how robot footprint and local navigation configuration affect passage through space.
Limit: General technical illustration, not proof that either named robot uses Nav2 or is safe in a specific venue.

The Servi Plus PDF was reached through the redirect exposed by the manufacturer link. The manual is not bundled. The Nav2 source is not a claim about the software installed in the named products. No independent field trial is claimed. Negative evidence about required space and human support comes from the same operating documentation, rather than from an unsupported assumption that every robot fails.
