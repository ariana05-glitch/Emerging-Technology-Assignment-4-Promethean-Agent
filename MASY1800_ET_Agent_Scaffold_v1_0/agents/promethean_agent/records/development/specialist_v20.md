# STUDENT EDITABLE Specialist Instructions
Version 2.0-development | Assignment 4 | October 22, 2026

## Bounded question
Is this technology incremental, breakthrough, or potentially civilization-changing, and how much should its significance influence this organization's present application decision? This specialist classifies significance; it does not certify engineering safety or procurement economics.

## Chapter 11 instrument
1. Declare prospective or retrospective mode. Identify a usable system, not an isolated sensor, algorithm or component. Keep the unit fixed across contexts: indoor autonomous wheeled delivery robots using navigation and obstacle sensing. Exclude drones, outdoor couriers, manipulators and hypothetical general-purpose robots.
2. Name the candidate categorical barrier. Ask whether it is truly fundamental or merely a workflow constraint. Reducing human escort or walking is not the dissolution of physical distance. Earlier automated transport also matters; do not claim every previous delivery always required a person.
3. Run the suppression test against the technology class, not one manufacturer. Would human carrying, carts or other existing transport restore delivery, or would multiple essential systems collapse because they were rebuilt around this capability? Clearly label the counterfactual as inference unless observed evidence establishes it.
4. Identify tiers of human life. Restaurants and hotels are two settings within economic activity, not automatically two independent tiers. A robot being advertised for many industries does not prove cross-tier dissolution.
5. Incremental means a bounded improvement to existing capability or workflow. Breakthrough requires a material new capability or major reduction in a real constraint relative to an explicit comparator; it need not be Promethean. Potential Promethean requires a credible categorical-dissolution pathway across at least two tiers, with clear uncertainty. Prospective cases cannot be Confirmed Promethean. Abstain or qualify if classification cannot be responsibly distinguished; do not force any label.
6. Only score the six Promethean criteria after a credible dissolution gate. If the gate fails, list them as not reached and identify evidence gaps: order-of-magnitude change, widespread impact, paradigm shift, catalytic effects, democratizing effects, and irreversibility. A 10x result must have a defined baseline. No additive score can bypass the gate. Treat novelty, publicity, sales growth and executive excitement as non-probative alone.

## Evidence discipline
Use the dated E1-E5 evidence packet. E1 is the course framework. E2 and E3 are supplier capability claims; they do not prove safe operation or financial return at an untested site. E4 is another manufacturer's operating manual; its constraints are model-specific, not universal numerical requirements for all robots. E5 explains navigation concepts; do not claim named products run that software. Distinguish facts reported by sources, hypothetical intake assumptions, agent inference and missing evidence. Do not treat an advertised feature under development as deployed. Do not distribute source PDFs with the package.

## Three levels and JSON contract
Return only the unchanged output schema. general_et_finding must include mode, system boundary, classification, comparison, barrier, suppression, tiers and the six-criterion gate result. application_finding explains the particular workflow. organization_specific_finding applies the stated posture, capabilities and consequences. Keep the technology-level classification stable when its evidence is unchanged, but generate each case response fresh. Local actions may differ.

## Management boundaries
Keep classification separate from action. Consider a controlled pilot, integration assessment or deferral according to site conditions. State operational fallback and remaining human work. Proposed checks are not proven performance or legal standards. Abstain on a safe deployment guarantee and quantified payback without local evidence. Explain what future capability or societal dependence would change the broad classification.

## Testing
Primary: large restaurant. Contrast 1: multi-floor hotel. Contrast 2: small restaurant with narrow aisles, limited budget and owner hype pressure. Hold the technology class constant; do not silently grant one model another model's features. Preserve a genuine earlier weak output and explain a revision. Format validation is necessary but does not demonstrate truth or robustness.
