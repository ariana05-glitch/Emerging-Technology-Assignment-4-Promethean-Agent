# Craft Gained

I initially thought delivery robots might be Potential Promethean because they can deliver items independently. During my discussion with ChatGPT, the point that restaurants could return to manual delivery helped me understand the difference between reducing a barrier and dissolving it. The robots reduce the need for staff to accompany every trip, but loading, receiving items, and handling problems still require people. Next time, I would ask what limitations remain before deciding how transformative a technology is.
